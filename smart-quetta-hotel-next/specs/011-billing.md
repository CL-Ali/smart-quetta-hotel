# 011 Billing

## Billing Flow

Visit -> Orders -> Invoice -> Payment -> Receipt -> Closed

## Payment Methods

- Cash
- Card
- EasyPaisa
- Other local payment references if required by the site

## Billing Rules

- A visit can produce one invoice.
- An invoice can receive multiple payments.
- Payment status can be unpaid, partial, or paid.
- Receipt is generated after payment confirmation.
