# 001 Requirements

## Product Scope

- LAN-first restaurant ordering
- Captive portal entry
- Guest identity and visit lifecycle
- Ordering, kitchen, billing, payments
- Receipt generation and recovery
- Admin and reporting

## Functional Requirements

- Guest ordering
- Kitchen order processing
- Cashier billing
- Reports and summaries
- Admin management
- Realtime updates
- LAN-first access
- Offline browsing and resilience

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Response time under 2 seconds for common actions
- Support at least 100 concurrent users on LAN
- Docker-based deployment
- Must work without public internet after setup
- Validation, auditability, and basic security controls
