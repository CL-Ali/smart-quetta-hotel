# 000 Vision

## Goal

Build a LAN-first restaurant ordering system that keeps the core business running even when internet is unavailable.

## Product Name

Smart Restaurant LAN Ordering System

## Core Flow

Customer connects to WiFi.

Captive portal opens automatically.

Guest identity is created.

Visit is opened.

Menu opens.

Customer places unlimited orders.

Kitchen receives orders in real time.

Cashier generates invoice and receipt.

Internet remains optional.
