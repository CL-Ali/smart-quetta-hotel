# 026 Printing

## Purpose

Define the printable artifacts for kitchen and billing.

## Documents

- Kitchen ticket
- Invoice
- Receipt

## Kitchen Ticket

- Ticket number
- Guest name
- Visit reference
- Item list
- Requested notes
- Timestamp

## Invoice

- Invoice number
- Visit reference
- Ordered items
- Tax and totals
- Payment summary

## Receipt

- Receipt number
- Invoice reference
- Paid amount
- Remaining balance
- Payment method
- Timestamp
