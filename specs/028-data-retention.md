# 028 Data Retention

## Purpose

Define what stays in the system and what can be archived.

## Retention Categories

- Active visits
- Closed visits
- Invoices
- Receipts
- Payments
- Audit events

## Rules

- Closed visits may be archived but not deleted immediately.
- Payment and receipt history should remain available for reporting.
- Session recovery data may expire earlier than financial records.
- Backups should preserve invoices, payments, and receipts.
