# 030 Glossary

## Terms

### Guest

A person using the restaurant ordering system during one or more visits.

### Visit

A temporary dining session that groups orders, billing, and settlement for one guest.

### Browser Session

The browser-local identity and state used to resume a guest flow on a device.

### Order

A request sent to the kitchen for one visit.

### Order Item

A single line within an order, usually a menu item with quantity and state.

### Invoice

The bill issued for a visit, containing totals, payments, and closure status.

### Payment

A recorded settlement against an invoice.

### Receipt

Proof that payment was accepted and the invoice was settled or partially settled.

### Ticket

A human-readable or machine-readable reference used to resume a visit.

### Kitchen Queue

The live list of orders or items waiting to be prepared, accepted, or served.

### Captive Portal

A network entry page that redirects a customer device into the restaurant app.

### Portal Adapter

A router-specific integration layer that translates captive portal behavior into app actions.
